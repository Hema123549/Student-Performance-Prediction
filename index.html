Model Benchmarking & Performance Evaluation

In Step 4 of train_model.py, two regression algorithms are evaluated on an 80% train / 20% test dataset split to determine the optimal candidate for production deployment.

✓ Selected Candidate

1. Linear Regression Model

Fits a continuous linear hyperplane expressing student final exam score as a weighted sum of raw and engineered features.

R² Score 0.9650
RMSE 2.15
MAE 1.82
Deployment Rationale: Linear Regression achieved a superior $R^2$ accuracy score and minimized mean absolute error, providing smooth continuous generalization across test samples.

2. Decision Tree Regressor (max_depth=5)

Splits feature space into hierarchical decision nodes to predict continuous student performance scores.

R² Score 0.8920
RMSE 3.84
MAE 3.10
Evaluation Assessment: Decision trees exhibited step-function decision splits, which slightly reduced continuous precision on smaller training sample sizes.
